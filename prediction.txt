WEEK 5 PREDICTION — dated 2026-09-17, written before any fix is applied.

TARGET MODE
  Mode 1 — "Refuses a one-line question instead of asking which recipe is
  meant." Baseline frequency: 3 of 20 traces = 15% (seed 20260917 sample;
  example traces 8c2b5e88 "Can I make it faster?", 4b3ae3f7 "How much salt?",
  7c7023dc "Is it done?").

THE CHANGE I WILL MAKE
  One change, in the grounding prompt only (prompt_version v2): add a
  clarify branch ahead of the refusal rule — when the question names no
  recipe and the retrieved chunks come from more than one recipe, the app
  must ask which recipe is meant and list the candidate recipe titles,
  instead of emitting the refusal sentence. Retrieval, chunking, k and the
  model stay untouched.

WHAT I EXPECT, IN NUMBERS
  On a FRESH seeded 20-trace sample drawn after the change:
  1. Mode 1 drops from 15% (3/20) to under 5% (0 or 1 of 20).
  2. Correct out-of-corpus refusals do NOT regress: questions whose answers
     are genuinely absent (biryani, microwaving idlis, white film on pickle)
     still produce the exact refusal sentence, so the A3_refusal_exact
     assertion stays at 100% on out-of-corpus cases.
  3. Invented quantities do not appear: A4_no_invented_grams stays at 100%.

HOW THIS CAN BE FALSIFIED
  If mode 1 stays at or above 10%, the prediction is wrong and the diagnosis
  ("the refusal rule fires before any clarify path exists") was wrong.
  If mode 1 drops but out-of-corpus refusals fall below 100%, the change
  traded a real safety property for a convenience one and must be reverted.

WHAT I AM NOT PREDICTING
  Modes 3, 4, 5 and 6 are untouched by a clarify branch. Mode 4 (comparison
  across cards) in particular cannot be fixed by any prompt or retrieval
  change; it needs computation over the tables.
