# Kadumanga Achar (Kerala fermented tender mango pickle)

Percentages are relative to the mango weight (100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | % of mango |
|---|---|---|
| Tender baby mangoes (kannimanga), whole | 1000g | 100% |
| Crystal sea salt | 150g | 15% |
| Kashmiri chilli powder | 50g | 5% |
| Gingelly (sesame) oil | 60g | 6% |
| Mustard seeds | 10g | 1% |
| Fenugreek powder, roasted | 5g | 0.5% |
| Asafoetida (hing) | 3g | 0.3% |

## Method

Wipe the mangoes dry — no water must touch them at any stage. Layer them
with the crystal salt in a sterilised bharani (ceramic pickle jar), close,
and shake once daily. Over 10 to 14 days the salt draws out the mango's own
brine and the fruit ferments in it, turning olive-drab and pleasantly tangy.
Then heat the gingelly oil, crackle the mustard seeds, switch off the heat,
and stir in the chilli powder, fenugreek powder and asafoetida. Cool the
spiced oil completely and fold it through the mangoes and their brine.
Return everything to the bharani. The pickle is eatable in a week but
matures over a month; it keeps a year at room temperature if only dry
spoons ever enter the jar.

## Allergen note

Contains sesame (gingelly oil) and mustard. Most asafoetida is bound with
wheat flour — use a certified gluten-free hing or omit it for gluten-free
diets; as written this card is NOT gluten-free.
