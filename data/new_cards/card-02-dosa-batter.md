# Crisp Dosa Batter

Percentages are relative to the rice weight (100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | % of rice |
|---|---|---|
| Dosa rice (raw, short-grain) | 750g | 100% |
| Whole white urad dal | 250g | 33% |
| Chana dal | 30g | 4% |
| Fenugreek seeds | 8g | 1% |
| Thick poha (flattened rice) | 50g | 6.7% |
| Fine sea salt | 18g | 2.4% |

Rice to dal ratio: 3:1. The chana dal gives colour, the poha gives crispness.

## Method

Soak rice, urad dal, chana dal and fenugreek together for 5 hours; soak the
poha separately for the last 30 minutes. Grind everything to a smooth, flowing
batter — thinner than idli batter — adding water a little at a time. Stir in
the salt and ferment 10 to 14 hours at 26–30°C until risen by half and sour-
smelling. Thin with water to pouring-cream consistency. Heat a cast-iron tawa
until a water drop dances, wipe with an oiled onion half, pour a ladleful in
the centre and spiral outward with the ladle base to a thin, even round.
Drizzle the edges with gingelly oil and cook until deep gold and crisp; a
proper dosa releases itself from the pan.

## Allergen note

Vegan and gluten-free as written. Gingelly (sesame) oil is used on the tawa —
substitute a neutral oil for sesame allergy.
