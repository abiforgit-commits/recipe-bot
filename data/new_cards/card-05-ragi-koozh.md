# Ragi Koozh (fermented finger-millet porridge)

Percentages are relative to the ragi flour weight (100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | % of ragi |
|---|---|---|
| Ragi (finger millet) flour | 300g | 100% |
| Broken rice (noi arisi) | 100g | 33% |
| Water | 1500g | 500% |
| Rock salt | 12g | 4% |

Serve with: sliced shallots, green chilli, and curd chilli (mor milagai).

## Method

Whisk the ragi flour into 500g of the water until lump-free, and leave the
slurry overnight — 10 to 12 hours — in a clay pot at room temperature to
ferment; by morning it smells pleasantly sour and shows fine bubbles. Cook
the broken rice in the remaining 1000g of water until completely soft. Stir
the fermented ragi slurry into the simmering rice in a steady stream,
whisking constantly, and cook 8 to 10 minutes until the porridge thickens
and turns glossy chocolate-brown. Add the rock salt at the end. Cool to
lukewarm and thin with water to a drinkable consistency. Traditionally
enjoyed the next day, when a second, gentler fermentation in the pot deepens
the sourness — this is summer street food, sold cold from mud pots.

## Allergen note

Vegan and gluten-free as written. Often served with buttermilk stirred in —
that version contains dairy.
