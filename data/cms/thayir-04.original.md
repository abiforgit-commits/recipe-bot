# Thayir — Homemade Curd (set overnight) — version: original

Percentages are relative to the milk weight (100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | % of milk |
|---|---|---|
| Whole milk (full-fat) | 1000g | 100% |
| Live curd from the previous batch | 30g | 3% |

## Method

Bring the milk just to a boil, then simmer 5 minutes to concentrate it
slightly — this gives a thicker set. Cool to 40–43°C: the milk should feel
distinctly warm, not hot, on a clean fingertip held for ten seconds. Whisk
the starter curd until smooth, stir it through the milk, cover, and leave
completely undisturbed for 6 to 8 hours at around 30°C. In cold weather, set
the pot inside a switched-off oven with the light on, or wrap it in a thick
towel next to a hot-water bottle. The curd is set when it jiggles as one mass
and the surface releases a little clear whey. Refrigerate immediately once
set; over-fermenting at room temperature turns it sharply sour by evening.
Save 30g as the starter for tomorrow's batch.

## Allergen note

Contains dairy (milk). Vegetarian, not vegan. Gluten-free.
