# Chettinad Chicken Curry — version: original

A dark, peppery Karaikudi curry built on a freshly roasted masala.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken, bone-in curry cut | 1000g |
| Gingelly (sesame) oil | 45g |
| Shallots, sliced | 200g |
| Tomatoes, chopped | 200g |
| Ginger-garlic paste | 30g |
| Curry leaves | 2 sprigs |
| Turmeric powder | 2g |
| Salt | 12g |
| Water | 300g |
| Dried red chillies | 6g |
| Coriander seeds | 15g |
| Fennel seeds | 5g |
| Black peppercorns | 8g |
| Cumin seeds | 3g |
| Poppy seeds | 5g |
| Grated coconut | 50g |
| Kalpasi (stone flower) | 1g |

Yield: serves 4.

## Method

Dry-roast the red chillies, coriander, fennel, pepper, cumin, poppy seeds and
kalpasi over a low flame until fragrant, add the coconut and roast until
golden, then grind to a smooth paste with a little water. Heat the gingelly
oil, fry the curry leaves and shallots until deep golden, add the
ginger-garlic paste, then the tomatoes, turmeric and salt, and cook to a
thick masala. Add the chicken and sear 5 minutes, stir in the ground paste
and the 300g water, cover and simmer 25 minutes, then uncover and cook 10
minutes more, until the gravy clings and the chicken is tender and cooked
through. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

## Allergen note

Contains sesame (gingelly oil) and coconut.
