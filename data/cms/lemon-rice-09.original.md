# Lemon Rice (Chitranna) — version: original

A 20-minute tempered rice for leftover rice: sharp, bright and crunchy.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cooked rice, cooled | 600g |
| Lemon juice (about 2 lemons) | 40g |
| Sunflower oil | 30g |
| Peanuts, raw | 40g |
| Mustard seeds | 5g |
| Chana dal | 10g |
| Urad dal | 5g |
| Green chillies, slit | 2 |
| Curry leaves | 1 sprig |
| Turmeric powder | 2g |
| Asafoetida (hing) | 1g |
| Salt | 8g |

Yield: serves 3.

## Method

Spread the cooked rice on a plate to cool and separate the grains. Heat the
oil, add the mustard seeds and let them crackle, then the chana dal, urad
dal and peanuts, and fry until the dals are golden and the peanuts crisp.
Add the green chillies, curry leaves, asafoetida and turmeric, and fry 10
seconds. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the lemon juice and salt, then
fold the tempering through the rice until every grain is yellow. Ready in
about 20 minutes from cooked rice.

## Allergen note

Contains peanuts and mustard. Asafoetida is usually bound with wheat flour —
use a certified gluten-free hing for gluten-free diets.
