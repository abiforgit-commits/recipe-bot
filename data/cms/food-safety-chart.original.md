# Food-safety chart (test kitchen) — version: original

Safe minimum internal temperatures, measured with a probe thermometer in the
thickest part, away from bone. Colour and texture ("golden", "cooked
through", "juices run clear") are not proof of safety.

## Safe internal temperatures

| Food | Safe internal temperature | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| Chicken and all poultry (whole, pieces, minced) | 74°C (165°F) | none |
| Minced meat (beef, lamb, pork) | 71°C (160°F) | none |
| Whole cuts of beef, lamb, pork | 63°C (145°F) | 3 minutes |
| Fish and shellfish | 63°C (145°F) | none |
| Egg dishes | 71°C (160°F) | none |
| Leftovers and reheated dishes | 74°C (165°F) | none |

## Cooked rice

Cool cooked rice within 1 hour, refrigerate it, and use it within 1 day.
Reheat rice only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Spores of
Bacillus cereus survive cooking and multiply in rice left at room
temperature.
