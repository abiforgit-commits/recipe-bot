# Nankhatai (Indian shortbread biscuits) — version: reformulated-2025

A crumbly, cardamom-scented Indian shortbread, baked until the tops crack.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Maida (all-purpose flour) | 250g |
| Besan (gram flour) | 30g |
| Fine semolina (rava) | 20g |
| Powdered sugar | 120g |
| Ghee and cashew-oil blend (supplier SB-2025) | 120g |
| Green cardamom powder | 2g |
| Baking soda | 1g |

Yield: 24 biscuits.

## Method

Beat the fat blend and powdered sugar for 5 minutes until pale and fluffy.
Sift in the maida, besan, semolina, cardamom and baking soda, and bring the
dough together with your fingertips — do not knead, or the biscuits turn
hard. Chill the dough 20 minutes. Roll into 24 balls of about 22g, flatten
slightly, and score a shallow cross on each. Bake on a lined tray at 180°C
for 16 to 18 minutes, until the bases are pale gold and the tops have
cracked. Cool on the tray for 10 minutes before moving them — hot nankhatai
crumble at a touch.

## Allergen note

Egg-free and nut-free — a tiffin-box favourite. Contains gluten (maida,
semolina) and dairy (ghee).
