# Allergen notes

Curated facts the recipe cards leave out. They are NOT in the search index. When a
request is flagged for an allergen, the matching section is pinned into the context
(w11_app.py, prompt version p-v5 onwards), so the model can cite it as
`[allergen-notes::<allergen>::0]`. Each `## <allergen>` section is one chunk.

Added in Week 11 after trace e6fa2dbb1746: the ragi koozh card says "Vegan ... as
written", yet lists curd chilli on its "Serve with" line, and no card says what curd
chilli is made of.

## dairy

Dairy means milk and anything made from it: milk, curd (thayir), buttermilk (mor),
yoghurt, ghee, butter, cream and paneer. None of these is dairy-free or vegan.

Curd chilli (mor milagai) is green chilli soaked in salted curd or buttermilk and then
sun-dried, so it contains dairy. It is not dairy-free or vegan, even when a recipe card
lists it as a side or calls the dish vegan.

Coconut milk is made from coconut, not from animal milk, so it is dairy-free.
